title: Agent autonomy boundaries
---

Agents may act autonomously: read anything, draft anything, prepare work.

Agents need explicit approval for every outward effect: sending anything to a customer or the
public, anything that costs money, anything hard to reverse. Adjust these lines to your own
risk appetite — this policy is what an agent checks before acting.
